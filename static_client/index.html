GAME MODES

Multiple ways to experience the void. From tactical 5v5 to massive 200v200 warfare.

PRIMARY

CORE ASSAULT

The classic bomb-mode experience translated to space. Attackers hack the Core, defenders protect it. Best of 24 rounds with full economy system.

5v5 ~40 min Permadeath
STRATEGIC

SECTOR CONTROL

Fight for control of 3 strategic sectors. First to 300 points wins. Features respawns every 15 seconds for continuous action.

5v5 ~20 min Respawns
PURE COMBAT

ELIMINATION

Last team standing wins. No objectives, pure combat skill. Sector shrinks over time forcing intense final confrontations.

5v5 ~15 min Shrinking
EPIC SCALE

VOID WARFARE

Massive 200v200 territory warfare. 5 capture points across a 5000x5000 unit map. Ticket system with respawns at controlled points.

200v200 ~60 min 5 Points
